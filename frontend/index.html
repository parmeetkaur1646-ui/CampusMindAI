Official Library Code of Conduct & Borrowing Limits

Undergraduate Students Borrow up to 3 books for 14 calendar days. Renewal permitted once if no prior reservation exists.
Postgraduate Students Borrow up to 5 books for 21 calendar days. Access to dedicated research terminals.
Overdue Regulations Fine of ₹2 per day per volume. Reference books & periodicals are strictly for in-library reading.

About Chandigarh Group of Colleges, Landran

Established in 2001 in Sector 112, Greater Mohali, Punjab, CGC Landran is accredited with NAAC A+ Grade and recognized for academic excellence across engineering, management, pharmacy, computer applications, and biosciences. Affiliated with I.K. Gujral Punjab Technical University (IKGPTU), Jalandhar.
