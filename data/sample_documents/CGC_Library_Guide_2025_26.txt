# CGC LANDRAN - CENTRAL LIBRARY (KNOWLEDGE RESOURCE CENTRE)
## OFFICIAL STUDENT GUIDE & CODE OF CONDUCT (2025-2026)
Website: https://www.cgc.edu.in/library | Email: library@cgc.edu.in

1. Facilities & Infrastructure:
- Centralized, fully air-conditioned library spanning over 25,000 sq. ft.
- Automated circulation via Koha Open Source ILS and Barcode scanners.
- Over 40,000 physical volumes and 3,500 active titles.
- Digital Library wing with 60 dedicated multimedia computing terminals.

2. Subscribed Online Databases & Portals:
- IEEE Xplore: Over 5 million full-text technical articles and standards.
- DELNET: Access to Union Catalogues and inter-library loan across 5,000+ national institutions.
- National Digital Library of India (NDLI): Access to open repository with educational resources.
- ScienceDirect / Elsevier & SpringerLink: E-journals for biotechnology and pharmacy research.
- J-Gate: Social science, engineering, and management indexing portal.

3. General Lending Privileges:
- Undergraduate Students: 3 books for 14 calendar days.
- Postgraduate Students: 5 books for 21 calendar days.
- Faculty Members: 8 books for the semester duration.

4. Book Bank Scheme:
- Every semester, students can enroll in the Book Bank scheme to receive a complete set of required semester textbooks at a nominal institutional fee. Textbooks are returned at the end of semester exams.

5. Rules and Fines:
- Overdue books incur a fine of Rs. 2/- per day per volume.
- Defaced or lost books must be replaced with the latest edition by the borrower or reimbursed at double market price.
- Strict silence must be observed in the general reading hall.
